FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Send a custom request and we'll answer your specific questions.

What is the minimum price?

Projects start at 1,500 MAD. More complex websites can cost more depending on pages, features and functionality.

Do you build restaurant websites?

Yes. Restaurant websites can include menus, galleries, opening hours, location, contact details and reservation or ordering features.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. We can create storefront experiences for products and collections. Ecommerce functionality and integrations are quoted based on the requirements.

Can I request a custom website?

Absolutely. Use the custom request form and describe exactly what you want. There is no need to force your idea into a package.

Are the websites mobile-friendly?

Yes. Responsive design is included so the website adapts to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

How do I communicate with you?

Project requests are sent to the Thimot WebForge Discord project channel through the secure order system.
